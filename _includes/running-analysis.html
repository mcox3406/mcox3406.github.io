    Definitions and calculation methods

    Planned curves cover every scheduled date. Completed-plan curves sum prescribed distance, duration, and effort only for running days marked complete on or before today. They stop at today and are not measurements of actual mileage or time. Missing check-ins are unrecorded; a zero completed-plan total does not establish that no running occurred.

    Imported activity curves sum recorded running distance and moving time only within the plan dates. AM/PM runs stay separate in the activity history. A snapshot can be incomplete or stale; cumulative totals can bridge dates with missing data. Coverage counts include dates before today reported complete by the export. No actual effort index is calculated from imported pace or heart rate. Moving time excludes stopped time and differs from elapsed or prescribed duration.

    The effort index is the sum of planned running minutes × prescribed main-session RPE, in arbitrary units (AU). It is a descriptive comparison within this plan. The daily model combines warm-ups, intervals, and doubles under one RPE, so this index is not measured session-RPE load, a fitness score, or an injury-risk estimate. Foster et al. (2001) study session-RPE monitoring; that method requires session-specific duration and a rating of the entire session.

    Reported RPE uses only your saved main-session ratings on or before today; missing ratings remain missing. Weekly RPE is an unweighted mean with its sample count. Mean and peak weekly mileage use complete Monday–Sunday weeks; week-to-week percentage changes require two complete weeks and a nonzero prior total. The peak seven-day total uses consecutive calendar days entirely within the plan. Completion percentage considers scheduled running days before today, so today’s unfinished training does not count against it.

    Easy-time estimates use supplied easyMinutes, with the daily-RPE fallback described above when segment data are absent. Long-run share is the share of planned running distance assigned to long-run days. Rest and cross-training are excluded from running totals. All charts and exports use the selected distance units.
